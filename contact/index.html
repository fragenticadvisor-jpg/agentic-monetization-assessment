Fit

What I say yes and no to

Being specific about this saves us both a round of emails. None of it is a hard rule — if you think your situation is the exception, say so and say why.

Usually yes

  • Software businesses whose pricing model is breaking under AI, and who know it
  • PE and VC operating partners looking at commercial model risk across a portfolio
  • Executive teams that want the argument made properly to a room that disagrees
  • Podcasts and publications willing to go past the AI-is-transformative headline
  • Collaborators bringing real data or operator experience to the framework

Usually no

  • Vendor pitches and partnership approaches for AI tooling
  • Requests to review, endorse or promote a product
  • Unpaid speaking at commercial vendor events
  • Generic AI strategy work with no commercial model question in it
  • Recruitment approaches
