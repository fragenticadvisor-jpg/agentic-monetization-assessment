Methodology & sourcing

Every Agentic Layer Check is a public-signal read — built from a company's own pricing pages, product pages, and public statements, dated to when it was written, never from private briefings or company input. Companies covered are not consulted beforehand, not paid for inclusion, and inclusion doesn't imply a relationship with or endorsement of this work. Pricing pages change; where a company updates its packaging after a piece is published, that will be noted rather than silently edited away.
