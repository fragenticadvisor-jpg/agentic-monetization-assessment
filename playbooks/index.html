How are the playbooks delivered?+
Each playbook is a fully interactive HTML document you can open in any browser, use on mobile, and save as a PDF. They include interactive tools — tier builders, discovery frameworks, audit matrices — that you can work through with your team.
What does the advisor session involve?+
A two-hour 1:1 session with FR Advisory, working directly from your assessment results. The session covers live results walkthrough, custom pricing model design for your specific product, and the audit prep checklist actioned together. Followed by 30-day email Q&A.
We're early-stage — is this relevant for us?+
Yes — the best time to design the right commercial model is before you've signed 200 legacy customers onto the wrong one. The assessment and playbooks are relevant from Series A onwards. Earlier-stage companies benefit most from Playbooks 01 and 02.
We already have a consumption model — is this still useful?+
Absolutely. Many businesses have launched a consumption layer but haven't addressed the underlying infrastructure gaps, the GTM motion, or the customer trust layer. The assessment will surface which of the five dimensions need attention regardless of where you are in the transition.
Can I buy just one playbook?+
Yes. Each playbook is available standalone. If you have a clear gap in one dimension — your assessment will identify it — you can buy the relevant playbook directly. Email fragenticadvisor@gmail.com with the playbook number and we'll send it over.
