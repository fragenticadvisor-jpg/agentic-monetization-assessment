About

Fessal Rahman

Originator of the Agentic Commercial Model. Group Executive Commercial Director at The Access Group. Founder of FR Advisory.

Fessal Rahman is the originator of the Agentic Commercial Model framework and author of Dead Model Walking and Dead Gods Walking — a two-book series making the case for commercial operating model transformation in businesses deploying agentic AI.

He is Group Executive Commercial Director at The Access Group (Hg Capital), leading commercial operations across a portfolio of 20+ software businesses with over $1B in ARR. He is also the founder of FR Advisory, which advises PE- and VC-backed SaaS companies on agentic AI monetisation, pricing architecture, and commercial model transformation.

His career spans McKinsey, Bain, Cloudinary, Emarsys, Exasol, and Virgin Media O2 — twenty years of operator experience across enterprise software, strategy consulting, and portfolio commercial leadership.

His thesis: the commercial operating models that built SaaS were not designed for a world where AI agents are the buyer, the user, and the decision-maker. Most businesses haven't noticed yet. The ones that do, and act, will define the next decade of B2B.
