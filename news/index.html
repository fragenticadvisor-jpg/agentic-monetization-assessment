Agentic Commercial Model News

A weekly briefing on agentic AI product releases, MCP servers, and pricing and packaging moves — the developments that actually change what a commercial model has to account for. Curated, not aggregated, with a note on why each one matters.

Published weekly. Every item links to a primary source — vendor documentation or an official announcement, not secondhand coverage. A quiet week means fewer items, not padded ones.
MCP & Agent Interfaces21 Aug 2026

HubSpot ships a first-party MCP server with scope-gated CRM read/write access

A remote MCP server, separate from the in-product Breeze copilot, with admin-authorised scopes covering CRM objects and engagements.

This is the shape most categories will eventually converge on — a permissioned agent interface that sits apart from the chat copilot. If your CRM vendor doesn't have an answer to this yet, that's worth asking about directly.

MCP & Agent Interfaces21 Aug 2026

Metronome publishes @metronome/mcp with write access to contracts and usage ingestion

Bearer-token authenticated, bundled in Metronome's official Node SDK, exposing writes including contract creation, not just usage queries.

Billing is usually the last system anyone lets an agent touch. A metering vendor shipping write scopes here, not just read, is a signal the rest of the category will have to match it.

Pricing & Packaging21 Aug 2026

PROS divests its B2B CPQ and pricing line to Conga, repositions around airline retailing

PROS is no longer the B2B pricing-science vendor it was categorised as even a year ago — that product line now sits with Conga.

A reminder to re-check vendor category maps on a cycle, not just build them once. "Best-in-class B2B pricing software" lists compiled a year ago may be citing a product that no longer exists in that market.

PROS / Conga announcement →

MCP & Agent Interfaces21 Aug 2026

ActiveCampaign documents a first-party MCP server with real write actions

A per-account remote MCP server, generated under Settings → Developer, supporting writes across contacts, lists, tags, automations and campaign sends.

More than the rule-based automation layer this category is usually credited with. Worth revisiting any "automation-only, not agentic" assumptions about marketing automation vendors generally.
