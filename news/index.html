Agentic Commercial Model News

A weekly briefing on the commercial-model implications of agentic AI — pricing and packaging shifts, compensation and GTM signals, and earnings-call proof points. Curated, not aggregated, with a note on why each one matters for the model, not just the product.

Published weekly. Every item links to a primary source — vendor documentation or an official announcement, not secondhand coverage. A quiet week means fewer items, not padded ones.

MCP & Agent Interfaces07 Sep 2026

Docusign opens its MCP server to every AI agent, with real write actions

Docusign's Model Context Protocol server goes generally available worldwide on September 30, giving agents in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and Slack governed read/write access — through its Iris AI engine — to analyze terms and execute end-to-end agreement workflows, not just look up status.

This is a first-party MCP server for one of the highest-stakes systems in the deal cycle — check now which of your own contract and closing steps you'd actually want an agent executing unsupervised.

MCP & Agent Interfaces07 Sep 2026

Genesys adds an AI Control Plane and Orchestrator that route work between agents and humans

Genesys announced new agentic orchestration tools for Genesys Cloud, including an Orchestrator that assigns and moves work between AI agents, human staff and systems, and an AI Control Plane for centralized policy and observability over autonomous actions.

A dedicated governance layer for agent actions, not just a chat interface, is the packaging pattern to watch — expect other CX and GTM platforms to ship their own version of this control plane.

Genesys newsroom →

Pricing & Packaging07 Sep 2026

Atlassian adds a per-resolution meter for autonomous AI agent outcomes

Atlassian is expanding usage-based pricing across Rovo credits, automation steps and a new "AI agent resolutions" meter that bills per outcome when an agent autonomously closes a request without human escalation, effective December 3.

This is one of the cleanest outcome-based pricing lines from a major platform vendor to date — a concrete reference point if you're building or negotiating agent-driven service pricing yourself.

Atlassian blog →

Earnings & AI Signals07 Sep 2026

Asana: AI products now drive 25% of net new ARR, up from 17% last quarter

On its Q2 FY2027 earnings call, Asana said AI Studio and AI Teammates drove about 25% of net new ARR, up from 17% the prior quarter, and confirmed its new "Agentic Work Management" product launches self-serve in mid-September.

A jump like that in a single quarter is a real, board-level signal that agentic features are already moving expansion revenue — a useful benchmark to hold your own AI-attach numbers against.

MCP & Agent Interfaces21 Aug 2026

HubSpot ships a first-party MCP server with scope-gated CRM read/write access

A remote MCP server, separate from the in-product Breeze copilot, with admin-authorised scopes covering CRM objects and engagements.

This is the shape most categories will eventually converge on — a permissioned agent interface that sits apart from the chat copilot. If your CRM vendor doesn't have an answer to this yet, that's worth asking about directly.

MCP & Agent Interfaces21 Aug 2026

Metronome publishes @metronome/mcp with write access to contracts and usage ingestion

Bearer-token authenticated, bundled in Metronome's official Node SDK, exposing writes including contract creation, not just usage queries.

Billing is usually the last system anyone lets an agent touch. A metering vendor shipping write scopes here, not just read, is a signal the rest of the category will have to match it.

Pricing & Packaging21 Aug 2026

PROS divests its B2B CPQ and pricing line to Conga, repositions around airline retailing

PROS is no longer the B2B pricing-science vendor it was categorised as even a year ago — that product line now sits with Conga.

A reminder to re-check vendor category maps on a cycle, not just build them once. "Best-in-class B2B pricing software" lists compiled a year ago may be citing a product that no longer exists in that market.

MCP & Agent Interfaces21 Aug 2026

ActiveCampaign documents a first-party MCP server with real write actions

A per-account remote MCP server, generated under Settings → Developer, supporting writes across contacts, lists, tags, automations and campaign sends.

More than the rule-based automation layer this category is usually credited with. Worth revisiting any "automation-only, not agentic" assumptions about marketing automation vendors generally.
