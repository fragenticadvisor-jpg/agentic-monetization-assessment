Questions partners ask

What does each portco need to provide?

30 minutes for the diagnostic, a 30-minute call, and read access to current pricing, packaging and sales comp documentation. No data room, no systems access.

Who sees the results?

The sponsor sees the full portfolio view. Each management team receives its own scores and 100-day plan. Everything is covered by NDA as standard.

Is this pricing advice?

No. Pricing is one layer of nine. The benchmark covers the whole commercial operating model: organisation design, go to market, pricing, sales, compensation, metering, customer success, revenue architecture and commercial systems.

Can it support a deal, not just the existing portfolio?

Yes. The same diagnostic runs on a target as part of commercial due diligence, typically inside a week.

What happens after the readout?

Most sponsors act on the 100-day plans with their own teams. FR Advisory can support implementation on the highest-risk portcos if needed.
