The Agentic Commercial Model

Value-Realised Selling

A funnel is a shape that narrows and stops. It was the right shape when revenue locked at signature. Under consumption and outcome pricing the signature is not the revenue, it is permission to start earning it. Value-Realised Selling runs the whole loop instead: lead generation, through closed won, into ongoing value management, and back round to demand.
